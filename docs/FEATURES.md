# Features

## Dashboard (home)
- Weighted overall‑progress ring (domains weighted by real exam share).
- Exam countdown, current program week and focus.
- **Today's plan** — auto‑generated from the current week's topics & labs; tap to complete.
- **Study‑session timer** — start / pause / finish; logs the time as study hours. Survives reloads.
- **Pace** card — are you on track? Actual vs needed hours/week, hours remaining.
- **Review** card — weak / low‑confidence topics surfaced for revision.
- **Streak** with weekday dots, plus a "keep your streak" nudge.
- Quick actions and a floating quick‑log button.

## Topics
- Every blueprint topic as a card with its "exam angle", confidence rating (1–5), notes, bookmark, revision counter, and per‑topic tags (weak/strong).
- Search, domain filter, status filter, and sorts (schedule / weakest / A–Z).

## Labs
- **150 detailed labs** across all six domains — difficulty, time estimate, technology.
- Per‑lab status (to‑do / doing / done), time logged, reflections, and file attachments.
- Search and difficulty/status filters.

## Flashcards
- Flip cards for every topic's exam angle; filter by domain; shuffle.

## Notes
- A personal scratchpad — create/edit/delete with undo, auto‑save.
- Each note can be **linked to a topic**.

## Log
- Log study hours per day with quick‑add buttons; a contribution‑style heatmap; session history.

## Analytics
- Hours by week and month, confidence distribution, weak‑area list, KPIs, projected finish.

## Goals
- Weekly study target and unlockable achievement milestones.

## Themes & design
- **4 full themes** (Copper, Midnight, Ocean, Meadow) — complete palette swaps, not accent‑only.
- Light/dark aware, mobile‑first responsive layout, subtle premium motion (respects reduced‑motion).

## Platform
- **Offline‑first PWA** — installable to phone/desktop, works with no connection.
- **Backup / restore** to a JSON file; **reset** progress.
- 100% client‑side — no account, no server, private by default.
- Top‑level **error boundary** for graceful recovery.
