# Roadmap

Planned and candidate features. Priorities may change based on user feedback.
Nothing here is a commitment or a delivery date.

## Near term
- **Cloud sync (optional)** — opt‑in account to sync progress across devices.
- **Quiz mode** — authored multiple‑choice questions per domain with scoring.
- **Spaced repetition** — schedule topic reviews by confidence and recency.
- **Notes on topics inline** — surface a topic's linked notes on the Topics page.

## Mid term
- **CSV / Anki export** of notes and flashcards.
- **Custom study plans** — reorder weeks, set your own hour targets per domain.
- **More themes** and a theme editor.
- **Localisation** (i18n) scaffolding.

## Long term
- **Multi‑exam engine** — reuse the shell for other certifications from one codebase.
- **Shared progress / study groups.**
